Complaint Submission Guidelines

📌 Genuine Complaints

Only submit complaints related to public services. False or misleading complaints may be rejected after verification.

📝 Accurate Information

Provide correct personal details and describe your issue clearly so that the concerned department can process your complaint efficiently.

⏳ Resolution Time

Most complaints are reviewed within 3 to 7 working days. Complex cases may require additional time depending on the department.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Your personal information is kept confidential and is only shared with the authorized department responsible for resolving your complaint.

⚖ Policy Compliance

Complaints containing abusive language, fake information, or unrelated content may be removed without prior notice.
